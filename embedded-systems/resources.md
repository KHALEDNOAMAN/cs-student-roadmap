# Embedded Systems Resources

A curated list of high-quality, free resources to master embedded systems.

## 📺 YouTube Channels
- **[Ben Eater](https://www.youtube.com/user/eaterbc)**: Incredible series on building an 8-bit computer from scratch. Great for understanding low-level computing.
- **[Phil's Lab](https://www.youtube.com/c/PhilsLab)**: Excellent tutorials on PCB design (KiCad, Altium) and STM32 microcontroller programming.
- **[DroneBot Workshop](https://www.youtube.com/c/Dronebotworkshop)**: Beginner-friendly tutorials on Arduino, ESP32, sensors, and basic robotics.
- **[Fastbit Embedded Brain Academy](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCyM-q8i-xH3j0M-g7m3A5sQ)**: Deep dives into ARM Cortex-M architecture and real-time operating systems.
- **[Digi-Key](https://www.youtube.com/user/digikey)**: Specifically their introductory series on FreeRTOS and KiCad.

## 🎓 Online Courses (Free)
- **[MIT OCW: Circuits and Electronics](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-002-circuits-and-electronics-spring-2007/)**: The classic course for foundational electrical engineering concepts.
- **[edX: Embedded Systems - Shape The World (UTAustinX)](https://www.edx.org/course/embedded-systems-shape-the-world-microcontroller-i)**: One of the best structured courses using an ARM Cortex-M microcontroller.
- **[Coursera: Introduction to the Internet of Things and Embedded Systems](https://www.coursera.org/learn/iot)**: Good high-level overview of IoT.

## 📚 Books (Often available as free PDFs legally through university links)
- *Make: AVR Programming* by Elliot Williams - Fantastic transition from Arduino to bare-metal C programming.
- *The C Programming Language* by Kernighan & Ritchie - The absolute bible for C programming.
- *Mastering the FreeRTOS Real Time Kernel* - Official, free documentation and book provided by FreeRTOS.

## 💻 Simulators
- **[Wokwi](https://wokwi.com/)**: Excellent browser-based simulator for Arduino, ESP32, and Raspberry Pi Pico.
- **[Tinkercad Circuits](https://www.tinkercad.com/circuits)**: Great for absolute beginners learning basic electronics and Arduino.
- **Proteus (Trial/Educational)**: Advanced simulation for varied microcontrollers and PCB design.

## 🌐 Communities
- **Reddit**: [/r/embedded](https://www.reddit.com/r/embedded/), [/r/electronics](https://www.reddit.com/r/electronics/), [/r/arduino](https://www.reddit.com/r/arduino/)
- **Stack Exchange**: [Electrical Engineering Stack Exchange](https://electronics.stackexchange.com/)
- **Discord**: Many YouTuber channels (like Phil's Lab) have active Discord communities for troubleshooting and advice.
